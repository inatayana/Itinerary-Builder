# 💰 BUSINESS RULES & PRICING SPECIFICATION
**Version:** 1.1  
**Authority Level:** AUTHORITATIVE  
**Project:** Bali Car Charter

---

## 1. PRICING AUTHORITY & FORMULA
1. **Model Perhitungan:** Seluruh tarif Bali Car Charter dihitung **per kendaraan (per vehicle)**, BUKAN per orang (per pax).
2. **Inklusi Standar:**
   * Unit kendaraan dengan AC terawat
   * Pengemudi fasih berbahasa Inggris (*English Speaking Driver*)
   * Bahan Bakar Minyak (BBM / Petrol)
   * Biaya parkir dasar & air mineral kemasan
3. **Eksklusi Standar:** Tiket masuk destinasi wisata, tiket jalan tol khusus (Toll Bali Mandara jika diminta di luar rute bandara), makan pribadi traveler, dan tip driver (sukarela).

---

## 2. PAKET CHARTER HARIAN & OVERTIME

| Tipe Paket Charter | Maksimal Durasi | Wilayah Operasional Standar | Catatan |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Half-Day Charter** | 5 – 6 Jam / Hari | South Bali & Central Bali | Cocok untuk short trip / dinner / sunset |
| **Full-Day Charter** | 9 – 10 Jam / Hari | South, Central, East, North, West Bali | Paket paling populer untuk tour harian |

### Aturan Overtime:
* Toleransi keterlambatan: 15 menit.
* Biaya overtime per jam: **10% dari harga paket Full-Day** armada yang dipilih.
* Maksimal overtime: 3 Jam. Jika melebihi 3 jam, traveler dikenakan biaya paket Half-Day tambahan.

## 5. ATURAN SAFETY & AUTO-SPLIT ITINERARY

### 5.1 Batas Maksimal Destinasi & Waktu
1. **Batas Maksimal Destinasi:** Maksimal 4 destinasi per hari.
2. **Batas Waktu Operasional Driver:** Maksimal 10 jam per hari (termasuk waktu tempuh dan waktu berkunjung).
3. **Trigger Auto-Split:** Jika estimasi total waktu ($T_{day}$) > 10 jam atau jumlah destinasi > 4:
   * Sistem otomatis membagi itinerary menjadi **2 Hari (Multi-Day)**.
   * Total harga dikalkulasi sebagai: $\text{Harga Paket Full-Day} \times 2 \text{ Hari}$.

### 5.2 Seasonal Pricing Adjustments

| Season | Period | Multiplier | Description |
|--------|--------|------------|-------------|
| **High Season** | July - August, December - January | 1.3x | Peak tourist season |
| **Shoulder Season** | April - May, September - November | 1.15x | Moderate tourist season |
| **Low Season** | February - March, October | 0.9x | Off-peak tourist season |

| Step | Time | Description |
|------|------|-------------|

| **Final Payment** | 24 hours before charter | 70% balance |
