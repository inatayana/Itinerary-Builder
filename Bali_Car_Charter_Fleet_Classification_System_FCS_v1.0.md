# 🛵 FLEET CLASSIFICATION SYSTEM (FCS)

## Transportation Blueprint — Canonical Truth of Fleet Data v1.1

**Project:** Bali Car Charter — Bali Car Charters Project

## Purpose

Fleet Classification System (FCS) adalah standar kanonik untuk semua produk transportasi Bali Car Charter.
Ini mendefinisikan kategori kendaraan, kapasitas penumpang, kapasitas bagasi, tier kenyamanan, aturan pemesanan, dan validasi operasional.

## Core Principles

1. Kebenaran Data Armada
2. Standar Mobile-First OTA
3. Kategori Pertama, Model Kedua

## Bagasi (Luggage) Rules Detail

### Kapasitas Bagasi Standar

**Compact MPV (Avanza/Xenia/Veloz)**
- Penumpang: Maksimal 6 orang
- Bagasi:
  * **Tas Kabin:** 5 tas ukuran sedang
  * **Koper Besar:** 4 koper ukuran besar (60-80L)
  * **Total:** 5 + 4 = 9 bagasi

**Premium MPV (Innova Reborn/Zenix)**
- Penumpang: Maksimal 6 orang
- Bagasi:
  * **Tas Kabin:** 5 tas ukuran sedang
  * **Koper Besar:** 4 koper ukuran besar (60-80L)
  * **Total:** 5 + 4 = 9 bagasi

**Executive Van (Hiace Premio)**
- Penumpang: Maksimal 12 orang
- Bagasi:
  * **Tas Kabin:** 10 tas ukuran sedang
  * **Koper Besar:** 8 koper ukuran besar (60-80L)
  * **Total:** 10 + 8 = 18 bagasi

**Large Group Van (Hiace Commuter)**
- Penumpang: Maksimal 14 orang
- Bagasi:
  * **Tas Kabin:** 12 tas ukuran sedang
  * **Koper Besar:** 10 koper ukuran besar (60-80L)
  * **Total:** 12 + 10 = 22 bagasi

**Luxury MPV (Alphard/Vellfire)**
- Penumpang: Maksimal 5 orang
- Bagasi:
  * **Tas Kabin:** 5 tas ukuran sedang
  * **Koper Besar:** 4 koper ukuran besar (60-80L)
  * **Total:** 5 + 4 = 9 bagasi

### Rules Bagasi Khusus

1. **Koper Besar:** Ukuran maksimum 60x40x20cm
2. **Tas Kabin:** Ukuran maksimum 45x35x20cm
3. **Aksesibilitas:** Bagasi harus terpasang dengan aman

## Charter Rules & Policies

### Age Requirements
- **Driver:** Minimum 25 years old
- **Traveler:** Minimum 18 years old (under 18 requires guardian)

### Insurance & Coverage
- **Third Party Liability:** Comprehensive coverage
- **Driver Insurance:** Personal accident coverage
- **Vehicle Insurance:** Full coverage during charter

### Cancellation Policy
- **Standard:** 48 hours advance notice required
- **Penalty:** 50% of charter rate for late cancellation
- **No-show:** 100% of charter rate

## Operational Guidelines

### Daily Usage Limits
- **Maximum Hours:** 12 hours per day (including downtime)
- **Maximum Distance:** 300 km per day (varies by category)
- **Driver Work Hours:** Maximum 10 hours active driving
- **Rest Periods:** Minimum 1 hour per 4 hours driving

### Safety Protocols
1. **Pre-Trip Inspection:** Vehicle, documents, driver status
2. **Route Validation:** Check road conditions, weather, traffic
3. **Emergency Procedures:** Driver contact, emergency locations
4. **Communication Standards:** Radio + WhatsApp + phone
5. **Documentation:** Trip sheet, passenger details, payment receipt

## System Integration Points

### Integration with Booking Engine
- **Fleet API Endpoint:** `/api/v1/fleet/categories`
- **Pricing Validation:** Check FCS tier vs booking request
- **Capacity Check:** Real-time availability validation
- **Driver Assignment:** Match driver qualification to vehicle category

### Integration with Payment System (Xendit)
- **Service Fee:** Based on vehicle category
- **Driver Disbursement:** 100% of charter rate (minus platform fee)
- **Platform Fee:** 10% for management services
